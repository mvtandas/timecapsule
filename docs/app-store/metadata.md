## Description (max 4000)
```
Voorcap turns your memories into sealed moments, dropped in time.

Create digital "caps" — photos, messages, and content linked to a specific place and moment — then seal them until the date you choose. Come back later, at the right time and the right spot, to open what you left behind.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Drop caps on a map — pin a memory to the exact place it happened
• Seal to a future date — some moments are better rediscovered later
• Trails — string caps together into a journey others can follow
• Gatherings — collect memories from a group into one shared cap
• Whispers — leave a private note for one specific person
• Scrolls — long-form, multi-part stories
• Friends & streaks — keep the moments flowing with people you care about

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Sealed caps stay sealed — their contents are hidden until the moment you set.
You control who sees what: public, friends-only, or a single recipient.

SAFETY
Report or block anyone, any content. Blocked people can't see your caps or
reach you.

Voorcap is available in 11 languages.
```
